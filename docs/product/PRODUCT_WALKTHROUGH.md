# Product walkthrough

1. **Research Object:** choose the company; inspect Overview, Current Research View, Memories, Compare and Research Records.
2. **Goal and Scheme:** state the question and as-of date. Generate and review the exact Scheme before confirming. These are real operations, not presenter controls.
3. **AI Research:** inspect Lead/specialist tasks and planned versus actual paths. Correction addresses an issue; replan changes research work under explicit authority.
4. **Results A/B/C:** read the report, inspect financial review checks and their public input/process/result/verdict, then open available execution contributions.
5. **Recovery:** distinguish a provider timeout from a research defect. A same-Run fallback may execute only after policy approval and within remaining budgets.
6. **Memory:** after release, inspect persisted verified metrics/claims and resolved issues. NOT_OBSERVED categories are not invented.
7. **Incremental study:** retain the prior released knowledge baseline, confirm the new intent, refresh/revalidate/prevent and compare the new released view.

The accepted screenshot lineage uses a released initial research (v1), historical failed attempts and a later released execution (v2). Knowledge lineage and execution-attempt lineage are distinct. The five images in [the gallery](screenshots/PROVENANCE.md) are actual persisted results, not a live Run executed for this documentation.

Report source/contribution mapping is partial; a complete Claim Trace drawer and deferred PDF are not available. Financial verification is bounded, not blanket validation of investment suitability.
