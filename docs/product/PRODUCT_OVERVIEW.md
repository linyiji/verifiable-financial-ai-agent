# Product overview

[简体中文](PRODUCT_OVERVIEW.zh-CN.md)

Verifiable Financial Agent (向风行 AI) turns a research objective into coordinated financial research with inspectable results and durable memory.

**Make It Useful:** confirm a model-backed Scheme, then let the Research Lead and specialist Agents execute a dependency-aware task graph, correct local issues and replan when warranted.

**Make It Trustworthy:** supported claims bind to evidence, deterministic calculations, financial checks, bounded proof and observable execution identities. Recovery decisions have their own policy and attempt evidence. Partial mappings stay visibly partial.

**Make It Better With Use:** released research becomes typed memory. Incremental research refreshes time-sensitive data, revalidates conclusions and prevents known mistakes; the next released view is compared against its knowledge baseline. This is implemented reuse, not online model training.

Designed for invited researchers, analysts and technical evaluators. Status: Local Deployable Alpha / Controlled Alpha Ready. It is not investment advice, public SaaS or enterprise readiness.
